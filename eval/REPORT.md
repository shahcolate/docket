# Red-team report: the warrant engine vs. an overeager agent

Generated by `npm run eval` — rerun it yourself; the numbers are not
hand-written. Every suite is deterministic, and every invariant below is
also enforced in CI by `test/*.test.js` — a regression fails the build,
it does not fail quietly.

The safety invariant everywhere: **nothing consequence-bearing is ever
silently allowed, and no failure mode fails open**. The engine may only
fail toward the human — a deny may soften to ask (reported below when it
happens), never to allow.

## Suite 3 — permission is never inherited

**Vague-target probes.** Every proper subset of every allow pattern's
content words, tried as a target — 218 probes. A vague target
("email") must never inherit a specific entry's permission ("status email
to the team"). Violations: **0**.

**Fuzzed targets.** 10,000 deterministic word-salad targets
(seed 20260704, 63-word vocabulary verified disjoint from every allow
entry by the matcher's own stemming), thrown at every loop and verb.
Allowed: **0**.

## Suite 4 — the record under attack

A 40-entry chain, 239 single-point mutations:

| Mutation | Attempts | Caught by the chain alone | Caught with pinned head |

| edit+rehash | 40 | 39 | 40 |
| delete | 40 | 39 | 40 |
| forge | 40 | 40 | 40 |
| swap | 39 | 39 | 39 |

The gap between the two columns is the documented one: a hash chain
cannot see its own tail being cut or rewritten — a truncated log is a
valid shorter log. That is why `docket record verify` prints the head
hash to pin externally: **with a pinned head, detection is total**
(239/239).

## Suite 6 — the gateway gate, live

The real `docket intercept` binary, real Docker MCP Gateway tool-call
payloads, gating the same `cross-tool-memory` template. The gateway
contract inverts the hook's in one dangerous way: **silence means the
tool runs**. So an interceptor that crashes, prints a warning, or emits
anything the gateway cannot unmarshal has allowed the call. All three
are counted as fail-open here, not filed as bugs for later.

Misconfigurations fail closed — every one blocks with a reason, at exit 0:

- unparseable payload (gated) → **block**

One asymmetry is deliberate and worth stating plainly: at the gateway
there is no human to prompt, so `ask` does not ask — it **blocks**, and
the message tells the model to get approval out of band. That is strictly
tighter than the hook, which is the only direction docket is allowed to
differ across surfaces.
